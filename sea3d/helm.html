      How you steer

      The autopilot's ghost sails the same boat through the same weather and the same bad luck. It never shows you what's ahead of your clock. Finish more than a day ahead of it to win; within a day is a draw, because that much is down to the weather's luck.

      Finish

      The finish circle is 330 km out to sea, too far to see land from. So this is a view of where the boat was headed, not where it is: Cascais, near Lisbon, with Cabo da Roca (Europe's westernmost point) and the Sintra hills to the north-west. The boat itself is still out at the finish.
