How you steer

The autopilot's ghost sails the same boat through the same weather and the same bad luck. It never shows you what's ahead of your clock. Finish more than a day ahead of it to win; within a day is a draw, because that much is down to the weather's luck.

The defaults are fine: just press Set sail. Time then runs by itself; change its speed or pause at the top of the screen. Keys: Space pauses, 1 to 4 set the speed, the arrow keys steer and set the power.
