    Practice sailing

    Breeze:

    Practice only: your times stay in this browser and never count toward a voyage or the leaderboard. The depths come from a survey model of the Bay, not a chart, and the tide isn't in it.

    Take the helm: a whole crossing

    Four days later: the Bay mouth

    Your crossing starts here, off the mouth of the Chesapeake Bay. The trip down the Bay is skipped and doesn't count toward your time.

    Take the helm

    Steer a boat from off the Chesapeake Bay to the finish off Portugal, through the real weather of 2020, against the autopilot sailing the same boat beside you.

    Your boat

    More options

    A challenge brings its own date and weather, so it replaces the launch day.

    How you steer

    The autopilot's ghost sails the same boat through the same weather and the same bad luck. It never shows you what's ahead of your clock. Finish more than a day ahead of it to win; within a day is a draw, because that much is down to the weather's luck.

    The defaults are fine: just press Set sail. Time then runs by itself; change its speed or pause at the top of the screen. Keys: Space pauses, 1 to 4 set the speed, the arrow keys steer and set the power.

    Loading the simulator...
    It runs in your browser: about 30 MB the first time.
    Finish

    The finish circle is 330 km out to sea, too far to see land from. So this is a view of where the boat was headed, not where it is: Cascais, near Lisbon, with Cabo da Roca (Europe's westernmost point) and the Sintra hills to the north-west. The boat itself is still out at the finish.
