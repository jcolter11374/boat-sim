Take the helm

You steer a boat from the drop point off the Chesapeake Bay to the finish off Portugal, through the real weather of 2020. Choose a heading and a power, then let time run in hops.

Your boat

The autopilot sails the same boat, through the same weather and the same bad luck, beside you as a ghost. It never shows you what's ahead of your clock. Finish more than a day ahead of it to win; within a day is a draw, because that much is down to the weather's luck.
