Race two designs

Two boats, the same launch, the same weather and the same luck, side by side. One race is one roll of the dice; the season shows which design is really better.

Start the race puts both boats in the water off the Chesapeake Bay (the first time, the page also downloads the simulator, about 30 MB). A crossing takes two to five months of sea time: sail it a day or a week at a time, stop when something happens, or skip to the end, which takes about half a minute. One race is one roll of the dice, so "Race all 6 launches" sails both boats from every 2020 launch date; that takes a few minutes.
