How to read this

Finished means arriving within 25 km of our target on the Microtransat finish line, west of Lisbon, under the race's rules. The thin bar is the range the true rate probably sits in (95%); with about 65 launches it is wide.

Tiers. Every design sails the same launches with the same weather and the same breakdown dice, so differences are the design, not luck of the draw. A design drops to a new tier only when it is clearly worse than every design above it. Designs in the same tier are too close to call.

Against the plan kayak counts the launches where one boat finished and the other didn't. "Too close to call" means the gap could be chance.

Hidden launches are a separate year nobody tunes against. A design that does much worse there than on the public launches gets a warning: it may be tuned to the test.

"Our model of" marks the Randolph team's boats as we simulate them, not their own results. Our kayak is 3.05 m long, over the race's 2.4 m limit, on purpose.
