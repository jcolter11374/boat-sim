How to read this

Finished means reaching the finish (a 25 km circle on the race's finish line, about 330 km west of Lisbon) under the race rules. The thin bar is the likely range: where the true share probably sits. With about 65 launches it is wide.

Groups. Every build sails the same launches with the same weather and the same breakdowns (parts failing at random), so differences are the build, not luck of the draw. A build drops to a new group only when it is clearly worse than every build above it. Builds in one group can't be told apart.

Against our kayak (the 3 m solar-electric kayak as we plan to build it) counts the launches where one boat finished and the other didn't. "Could be luck" means the gap is too small to be sure of.

Show the statistics adds the likely ranges and the hidden year: a separate year nobody tunes against. A build that does much worse there than on the main launches gets a warning: it may be tuned to the test.

"Our model of" marks Randolph's team's boats (Rev B, Rev C) as we simulate them, not their own results.
